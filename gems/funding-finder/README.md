# Funding Finder

## Description

Helps researchers identify UK grant and funding opportunities that match their project, career stage, and discipline. Not limited to any single subject area — covers UKRI research councils, Innovate UK, Research England, and major independent/charitable funders (Wellcome Trust, Leverhulme Trust, Royal Society, British Academy, Nuffield Foundation, and others). Points researchers to authoritative sources to confirm current deadlines and eligibility rather than inventing details.
